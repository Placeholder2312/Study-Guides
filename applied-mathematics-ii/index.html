Applied Mathematics-II

University of Mumbai · B.E. Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning · NEP 2020

Course 10521 · Semester II · 6 syllabus modules
This is the Semester II Applied Mathematics-II course. It is different from the existing Semester III subject, Mathematics for Computer Engineering (2023111); both remain in their respective semesters.

Module 1

Differential Equations of First Order and First Degree

Exact differential equations and equations reducible to exact form using integrating factors; linear differential equations, equations reducible to linear form and Bernoulli’s equation.

Module 2

Linear Differential Equations with Constant Coefficients of Higher Order

Complementary functions and particular integrals for standard right-hand sides; variation of parameters. Cauchy’s homogeneous and Legendre differential equations are listed as self-learning topics.

Module 3

Beta and Gamma Function; Differentiation under Integral Sign

Beta and Gamma functions and their properties; differentiation under the integral sign with constant integration limits.

Module 4

Multiple Integration – I

Double integrals in Cartesian and polar coordinates; changing the order of integration (without evaluation); evaluation after conversion to polar coordinates.

Module 5

Multiple Integration – II

Triple integrals in Cartesian, cylindrical and spherical-polar coordinates; applications of double integrals to area and mass. Triple-integral volume applications are listed for self-learning.

Module 6

Numerical ODE Solutions and Numerical Integration

Euler, Modified Euler and fourth-order Runge–Kutta methods for first-order, first-degree ODEs; trapezoidal, Simpson’s 1/3 and Simpson’s 3/8 numerical-integration rules (without proof).

Module 4 lists tracing of curves as a prerequisite. Self-learning topics are identified in the syllabus and are not silently presented here as core module subtopics.
