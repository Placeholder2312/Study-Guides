## Quotation Usage Policy
- Quotations are kept short and placed in bordered blocks with author, work, section and link.
- Surrounding explanation is the guide's own paraphrase or commentary, not the author's words.
- No quotation is invented, and no source is cited for a claim it does not make.
- Where a numerical answer depends on a scanned diagram, the assumption is printed with the answer.

## What This Page Does Not Include
- C-scheme 58654 papers (different course/scheme); labelled as such where mentioned.
- Teacher assignments or practice bank as university questions.
- Modules 2–6.
- Dependent sources, supernodes, supermeshes.
