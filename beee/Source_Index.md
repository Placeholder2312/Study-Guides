## Quotation Usage Policy
- Quotations are kept short and placed in bordered blocks with author, work, section or slide number, and a link where one exists.
- Surrounding explanation is the guide's own paraphrase or commentary, not the author's words.
- No quotation is invented, and no source is cited for a claim it does not make.
- Course slides are labelled `<span class="tag c">Course</span>` and never presented as university publications.
- Supplementary practice questions are written for the guides, labelled as such, and are not presented as university questions.
- Where a numerical answer depends on a scanned diagram, the assumption is printed with the answer (Module I only; Module III has no numericals).

## What These Pages Do Not Include
- C-scheme 58654 papers (different course/scheme); labelled as such where mentioned.
- Teacher assignments or practice banks as university questions.
- In Module I: dependent sources, supernodes, supermeshes.
- In Module III: three-phase transformers, parallel operation, phasor diagrams and equivalent circuits, design calculations, and full OC/SC test procedures.
- Any Module III assignment, tutorial or practice bank — none was supplied. This limitation is stated on the Module III page itself.
