Applied Physics

University of Mumbai · B.E. Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning · NEP 2020

Course 10412 · Semester I · 2 credits · 6 syllabus modules
Semester I theory subject. The module sequence below follows the University of Mumbai NEP 2020 syllabus for B.E. AI & ML.

Module I

Lasers

Spontaneous and stimulated emission, population inversion, pumping, active medium, resonant cavity, coherence, He–Ne and fibre lasers; applications including LiDAR, barcode readers and metal work.

Module II

Fibre Optics

Critical and acceptance angles, numerical aperture, total internal reflection, fibre types, attenuation, fibre-optic communication systems and numerical problems.

Module III

Interference in Thin Films

Interference in uniform-thickness films, maxima and minima in reflected light, wedge-shaped films, Newton’s rings and anti-reflecting coatings.

Module IV

Electrodynamics

Gradient, divergence and curl; Gauss’s law, Ampère’s circuital law, Faraday’s law, divergence and Stokes theorems, and Maxwell’s equations in point and integral forms.

Module V

Quantum Physics

de Broglie matter waves, wave function and probability density, Schrödinger equations, particle in a rigid box, quantum tunnelling and the qubit concept.

Module VI

Basics of Semiconductor Physics

Direct and indirect band-gap semiconductors, electrical conductivity, drift velocity, mobility, Fermi–Dirac distribution and Fermi-level positions in intrinsic and extrinsic semiconductors.

The prerequisite knowledge listed by the syllabus is not repeated as a numbered module here. Module labels I–VI are retained as printed in the syllabus.
