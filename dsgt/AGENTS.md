# DSGT Study Guide Project — Agent Rules

## Standing Rules

### 1. HTML Editing Safety
Before rendering or editing any HTML file, check tag balance for these tags: `div`, `p`, `span`, `strong`, `ol`, `ul`, `li`, `table`, `tr`, `td`, `th`, `section`, `header`, `footer`, `nav`. Look for mismatched closers such as `<strong>...</span>` or `<ol>...</ul>`. If a container div (such as the "practice" box) is unclosed, every section after it nests inside it and the margins break.

### 2. No Silent Edits
Never edit files until you have shown me the problem and I approve. Always present the issue and proposed fix before making changes.

### 3. Post-Edit Verification
After any change, verify the result. For HTML files, render them with headless Chrome and look at the output (PNG screenshots or PDF) instead of guessing from source code alone.

### 4. Prefer Small Edits
Prefer small, exact edits over rewriting whole files. Make targeted changes rather than replacing entire files.

### 5. Module Scope
This guide covers DSGT (Discrete Structures and Graph Theory) Module 4 (Counting) and Module 5 (Algebraic Structures). Do not modify Module 4 unless explicitly requested. Do not work on Modules 1, 2, 3, or 6 unless explicitly requested.

### 6. Question Bank Integrity
Do not invent additional questions merely to make the guide longer. Preserve all existing teacher-provided examples and syllabus content.

### 7. Mumbai University Exam Questions
Questions marked with the `.mu-badge` class are from verified Mumbai University end-semester examinations. Do not alter their wording, marks, or question numbers without explicit approval.

### 8. No AI Slop
Write explanations in plain, human language. Avoid AI-generated templated language. Do not claim formulas or examples come from sources you haven't verified.
