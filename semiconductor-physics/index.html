Semiconductor Physics

University of Mumbai · B.E. Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning · NEP 2020

Course 10552 · Semester II · Physics elective · 6 syllabus modules
This page maps the Semester II Physics elective Semiconductor Physics. It is the Physics course listed as 10552 in the B.E. AI & ML Semester II examination register; it is not the Semester I Applied Physics course.

Module 1

Basics of Semiconductors

Intrinsic and extrinsic semiconductors; carrier concentration; Fermi-level position and variation with temperature and impurity concentration; conductivity and current flow; Hall effect and Hall-voltage calculation.

Module 2

Junction Diode

Formation of a p–n junction, barrier potential, diode equation, forward and reverse bias, current–voltage characteristics and LED operation.

Module 3

Important Diodes

Operation and applications of the photodiode, solar cell, Zener diode, varactor diode and Gunn diode.

Module 4

Bipolar Junction Transistors

BJT structure and operating modes; common-base and common-emitter I–V characteristics; current gain; BJT use as an amplifier and switch.

Module 5

Field Effect Transistors

JFET and MOSFET types, structure and operation; enhancement and depletion modes, threshold voltage and MOSFET switching applications.

Module 6

Nanotechnology

Introduction and optical, electrical, structural and mechanical properties; surface-to-volume ratio; van der Waals bonding; lithography, single-electron transfer and spin valves.

Physics is an elective group in the Semester II scheme. The course catalogue also lists Physics for Emerging Fields and Physics of Measurements and Sensors as alternative Physics subjects.
