Engineering Mechanics

University of Mumbai · B.E. Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning · NEP 2020

Course 10414 · Semester I · 2 credits · 6 syllabus modules
Semester I theory subject. The prerequisite band is kept separate because the syllabus explicitly says no theory-examination questions are to be set from it.

Prerequisite · Not examined

Foundational mechanics

Force resolution and force laws; Lami’s theorem and vector algebra; straight-line and projectile motion; conservation of energy and momentum; elastic collision; work–energy principle.

Module I

System of Forces

Classification, transmissibility, composition and resolution of forces; resultants of coplanar concurrent, parallel and general force systems; moments, couples, Varignon’s theorem and concurrent space-force systems.

Module II

Centroid

Centroids of plane laminas made from primitive geometrical shapes.

Module III

Equilibrium of Force System and Friction

Conditions of equilibrium, rigid-body equilibrium and free-body diagrams; beam types, supports, reactions and loads; laws of friction, cone of friction, angle of repose and friction, and inclined-plane equilibrium.

Module IV

Kinematics of Particle and Rigid Bodies

Variable-acceleration motion, plane-curved paths, rectangular and tangential/normal components of velocity and acceleration; general plane motion using the instantaneous-centre method (up to two-linkage mechanisms).

Module V

Kinetics of Particle

Force and acceleration, D’Alembert’s principle, inertia force, dynamic equilibrium, linear impulse and momentum, impact and collision, coefficient of restitution and kinetic-energy loss in inelastic collisions.

Module VI

Introduction to Robot Kinematics

Fundamentals of robot mechanics, degrees of freedom, D–H parameters, forward kinematics and homogeneous transformation for a serial robot limited to 2 DOF.

The syllabus excludes the relative-velocity method for the stated general-plane-motion problems and excludes internal-hinge beam problems. Keep these restrictions in mind while choosing practice material.
